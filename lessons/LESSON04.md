## 💬 1. The Dialog (Diálogo)

| Interlocutor | Fala / Linha de Diálogo |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | *Hey! How's your week going?*<br> |
| **B** | *Pretty good! I've been quite busy, but I'm managing to **keep up with** everything.*<br> |
| **A** | *What does a typical day look like for you?*<br> |
| **B** | I usually **log in** around 9, check my emails, and then **jump on** a few meetings. After that, I **get down to** work on my main tasks.

 |
| **A** | *Do you work with a team?*<br> |
| **B** | Yes, we have a daily stand-up in the morning to **stay aligned**. In the afternoon, I often collaborate with the developers and help **iron out** any issues.

 |
| **A** | That sounds intense. Do you ever take breaks?

 |
| **B** | Definitely! I try to **step away** for a short break, maybe grab a coffee or go for a quick walk. It helps me **clear my head**.

 |
| **A** | *Nice! And how do you usually end your day?*<br> |
| **B** | I **wrap up** my tasks, update my to-do list, and **log off** around 6. Sometimes I still have a few things to finish, but I try not to **take work home**.

 |
| **A** | That's a good balance. I need to be better at that!

 |
| **B** | Yeah, it's important to **set boundaries**, right? Work is important, but so is your well-being.

 |

## 📚 2. Useful Vocabulary (Vocabulário & Expressões)

| 🏷️ Expressão / Idiom | 🎯 Significado (Meaning) | 💡 Exemplo de Uso (Example) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **be going (well)**<br> | To be progressing *(estar progredindo / indo bem)*<br> | *How's your project going?*<br> |
| **keep up with**<br> | Manage to do all tasks *(dar conta / acompanhar o ritmo)*<br> | *It's hard to keep up with all the meetings.*<br> |
| **log in**<br> | Connect to a system *(conectar-se / iniciar sessão)*<br> | *I log in at 9 every morning.*<br> |
| **jump on**<br> | Join a call or meeting *(entrar rapidamente em uma chamada)*<br> | *Let's jump on a call to discuss this.*<br> |
| **get down to**<br> | Start working on something *(começar a trabalhar a sério em algo)*<br> | *I need to get down to work now.*<br> |
| **stay aligned**<br> | Have the same understanding *(manter o alinhamento de ideias)*<br> | *We have a stand-up to stay aligned.*<br> |
| **iron out**<br> | Solve or fix problems / details *(aparar arestas / resolver pendências)*<br> | *We need to iron out a few details.*<br> |
| **step away**<br> | Take a break *(afastar-se por um momento / pausar)*<br> | *I'll step away for a few minutes.*<br> |
| **clear my head**<br> | Relax and feel calmer *(esvaziar a mente / desestressar)*<br> | *I went for a walk to clear my head.*<br> |
| **wrap up**<br> | Finish or conclude something *(concluir / finalizar)*<br> | *I'll wrap up my tasks before the meeting.*<br> |
| **log off**<br> | Disconnect from a system *(desconectar-se / sair do sistema)*<br> | *I usually log off at 6.*<br> |
| **take work home**<br> | Continue working outside hours *(levar trabalho para fora do horário)*<br> | *I try not to take work home.*<br> |
| **set boundaries**<br> | Establish healthy limits *(definir limites saudáveis)*<br> | *It's important to set boundaries between work and personal life.*<br> |

## 🧠 3. Reading Comprehension (Compreensão de Texto)

1. **How is B's week going?**

*(Como está sendo a semana da pessoa B?)*
2. **What time does B usually log in?**

*(A que horas B costuma se conectar?)*
3. **What does B do after checking emails?**

*(O que B faz depois de checar os e-mails?)*
4. **Why do they have a daily stand-up?**

*(Por que eles realizam uma daily stand-up?)*
5. **What does B do to take a break?**

*(O que B faz quando faz uma pausa?)*
6. **How does B end his/her day?**

*(Como B costuma encerrar o dia de trabalho?)*
7. **What does B say about taking work home?**

*(O que B diz a respeito de levar trabalho para casa?)*
8. **What is A's final comment?**

*(Qual é o comentário final da pessoa A?)*
