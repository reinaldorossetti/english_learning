## 💬 QA Team Dialogue & Daily Questions (Diálogo de QAs)

> 🎙️ **Contexto:** Alinhamento diário (Daily Standup) e sessão de triagem de bugs (Bug Triage) entre 👩‍💼 **Sarah (QA Lead)** e 👨‍💻 **Lucas (Senior QA Automation Engineer)** analisando os testes de regressão automatizados e preparando o corte de release para produção.  
> 💡 *Dica de estudo:* As perguntas em destaque utilizam as estruturas gramaticais detalhadas em [questions.md](questions.md). Pratique falando em voz alta com a fonética!

---

### 🎭 The Dialogue: Standup, Test Analysis & Bug Triage

**👩‍💼 Sarah (QA Lead):**
> *"Good morning, Lucas! **How did the overnight regression suite perform on the CI/CD pipeline?** Did we encounter any unexpected blockers?"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *gud mórnin, lúkas! ráu did dhâ ôuvernait rigréshân suít pârfórm on dhâ si-ái-si-dí páipláin? did ui encáuntâr éni anexpéctid blókârz?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Bom dia, Lucas! Como foi a execução da suíte de regressão noturna no pipeline de CI/CD? Encontramos algum bloqueador inesperado?  
> 📌 *Estruturas:* **How did + sujeito + verbo** (*Wh- question no passado*) e **Did we + verbo** (*Yes/No question no passado simples*).

**👨‍💻 Lucas (Senior QA Automation Engineer):**
> *"Morning, Sarah! Overall, 92% of the automated scenarios passed. However, **why did the checkout payment service fail during test step 4?** It seems that the integration mock timed out after thirty seconds."*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *mórnin, sérâ! ôuvâról, nainti-tú pârsént âv dhi ótômêitid sinériouz pæst. rauéver, uái did dhâ tchékaut pêimênt sârvis fêil diúrin tést stêp fôr? it síms dhét dhi intêgrêixân mók táimd áut éftâr thârti sékândz.*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Bom dia, Sarah! No geral, 92% dos cenários automatizados passaram. Porém, por que o serviço de pagamento do checkout falhou na etapa 4? Parece que o mock de integração deu timeout após 30 segundos.  
> 📌 *Estrutura:* **Why did + sujeito + verbo** (*Wh- question investigativa no passado simples*).

**👩‍💼 Sarah (QA Lead):**
> *"That sounds critical. **Can you reproduce this issue consistently in the staging environment?** Or is it just an intermittent flaky test?"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *dhét sáundz críticâl. kén iú riprôudiús dis íxiu cônsistêntli in dhâ stêidjin inváirônmênt? ór iz it djâst ên intârmítênt flêiki tést?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Isso parece crítico. Você consegue reproduzir esse problema com consistência no ambiente de staging? Ou é apenas um teste intermitente (flaky)?  
> 📌 *Estruturas:* **Can you + verbo** (*Modal para habilidade/possibilidade*) e **Or is it...?** (*Choice question alternativa*).

**👨‍💻 Lucas (Senior QA Automation Engineer):**
> *"I ran it three times manually via Postman and Cypress. The issue is persistent under high payload. **What was the root cause?** The auth token expires prematurely during asynchronous webhook processing. **Should I raise a blocker bug in Jira or reach out directly to the backend squad?**"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *ai rên it thrí táimz mêniuali váia pôustmên ênd sáiprês. dhi íxiu iz pârsístênt ândâr rái pêilôud. uót uâz dhâ rút cóz? dhi ót tôukân ekspáiârz primâtchúârli diúrin eisíncrônâs uébrúk prôuséssin. xud ai rêiz a blókâr bág in djíra ór rítch áut dairéctli tchu dhâ békend skuód?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Eu rodei três vezes manualmente via Postman e Cypress. O problema persiste com carga alta. Qual foi a causa raiz? O token de autenticação expira antes do tempo durante o processamento assíncrono do webhook. Devo abrir um bug bloqueador no Jira ou falar diretamente com a squad de backend?  
> 📌 *Estruturas:* **What was + substantivo** (*Wh- com verbo to be no passado*) e **Should I + verbo** (*Modal para pedir conselho/orientação*).

**👩‍💼 Sarah (QA Lead):**
> *"Please file the blocker ticket immediately with full HAR logs and stack traces. **Who is currently responsible for the payment gateway service in this sprint?**"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *plíz fáil dhâ blókâr tíkêt imídiêtli uíd fúl rár lógz ênd stæk trêisêz. rú iz cârêntli rispónsâbâl fôr dhâ pêimênt gêituei sârvis in dis sprint?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Por favor, registre o ticket bloqueador imediatamente com logs HAR completos e stack traces. Quem é atualmente responsável pelo serviço de gateway de pagamento nesta sprint?  
> 📌 *Estrutura:* **Who is + complemento** (*Wh- question identificando o sujeito*).

**👨‍💻 Lucas (Senior QA Automation Engineer):**
> *"Alex is the tech lead on that feature. **Could you tell me what time the release deployment window is scheduled for tonight?**"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *álêks iz dhâ ték líd on dhét fítchâr. cud iú tél mi uót táim dhâ rilís déplóimênt uíndou iz skédjuld fôr tunáit?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Alex é o tech lead dessa funcionalidade. Você poderia me dizer a que horas a janela de implantação da release está agendada para esta noite?  
> 📌 *Estrutura:* **Could you tell me what time [sujeito] is...?** (*Pergunta indireta e educada — ordem afirmativa no final!*).

**👩‍💼 Sarah (QA Lead):**
> *"The maintenance window opens at 8 PM UTC. **When will the bug fix be ready for validation in the staging cluster?** We need at least one hour for regression verification."*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *dhâ mêintênâns uíndou ôupânz ét êit pí-ém iú-ti-sí. uén uíl dhâ bág fiks bi rédi fôr válidêixân in dhâ stêidjin clástâr? ui níd ét líst uân áuâr fôr rigréshân vêrifikêixân.*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** A janela de manutenção abre às 20h UTC. Quando a correção do bug estará pronta para validação no cluster de staging? Precisamos de pelo menos uma hora para verificação de regressão.  
> 📌 *Estrutura:* **When will + sujeito + be + adjetivo** (*Wh- question no futuro simples*).

**👨‍💻 Lucas (Senior QA Automation Engineer):**
> *"Alex said the patch is being reviewed right now. **Have all acceptance criteria been fulfilled for the other tickets in the sprint?**"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *álêks séd dhâ pætch iz bíin riviúd ráit nau. rév ól akséptâns craitíria bin fúlfild fôr dhi âdhâr tíkêts in dhâ sprint?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** O Alex disse que o patch está sendo revisado agora. Todos os critérios de aceite foram atendidos para os outros tickets da sprint?  
> 📌 *Estrutura:* **Have all [sujeito] been [particípio]** (*Yes/No question no Present Perfect passivo*).

**👩‍💼 Sarah (QA Lead):**
> *"Yes, all user stories passed UAT. **You will monitor the CI/CD pipeline once the hotfix is merged, won't you?**"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *iés, ól iúzer stóriz pæst iú-êi-tí. iú uíl mónitâr dhâ si-ái-si-dí páipláin uâns dhâ rótfiks iz mârdjd, uôunt iú?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Sim, todas as histórias de usuário passaram no UAT. Você vai monitorar o pipeline de CI/CD assim que o hotfix for integrado, não vai?  
> 📌 *Estrutura:* **You will..., won't you?** (*Tag question para confirmação*).

**👨‍💻 Lucas (Senior QA Automation Engineer):**
> *"Absolutely! As soon as the green build is confirmed, I'll update the test execution dashboard and sign off on the quality gate. **Would you like me to send an email summary to the stakeholders as well?**"*  
> 🗣️ **Fonética:** *ábsôulútli! êz sún êz dhâ grín bild iz cônfârmd, ail apdéit dhâ tést éksêkiúxân dæxbôrd ênd sáin óf on dhâ cuóliti gêit. uúd iú láik mi tchu send ên ímeil sâmâri tchu dhâ stêikhôuldârz êz uél?*  
> 🇧🇷 **Tradução:** Com certeza! Assim que a build verde for confirmada, atualizarei o dashboard de execução de testes e aprovarei o quality gate. Você gostaria que eu enviasse um resumo por e-mail para os stakeholders também?  
> 📌 *Estrutura:* **Would you like me to + verbo** (*Modal de cortesia para oferecer ajuda*).

**👩‍💼 Sarah (QA Lead):**
> *"That would be awesome! Thanks, Lucas. Let's touch base again after the hotfix lands."*

## ❓ Essential QA Questions by Category (Perguntas Essenciais de QA)

Pratique estas perguntas frequentes no dia a dia de qualidade de software, organizadas pelos principais momentos do ciclo de testes:

### ☕ 1. Daily Standup & Sprint Planning

| 💬 Pergunta em Inglês | 🔊 Fonética Aproximada | 🇧🇷 Tradução | 📐 Tipo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What are we testing today?** | *uót ar ui téstin tudei?* | O que estamos testando hoje? | Wh- (Present Continuous) |
| **Are there any blockers preventing test execution?** | *ar dhér éni blókârz privéntin tést éksêkiúxân?* | Há algum bloqueador impedindo a execução dos testes? | Yes/No (To Be / Existencial) |
| **Have the user stories been refined yet?** | *rév dhâ iúzer stóriz bin rifáind iét?* | As histórias de usuário já foram refinadas? | Yes/No (Present Perfect) |
| **Who is picking up the test case creation for this epic?** | *rú iz píkin ap dhâ tést kêis criêixân fôr dis épik?* | Quem está assumindo a criação de casos de teste para este épico? | Wh- (Subject Question) |
| **Could we clarify the acceptance criteria for this edge case?** | *cud ui clérifái dhi akséptâns craitíria fôr dis édj kêis?* | Poderíamos esclarecer os critérios de aceite para este caso de borda? | Modal (Polite Request) |

### 🐛 2. Defect Triage & Bug Investigation

| 💬 Pergunta em Inglês | 🔊 Fonética Aproximada | 🇧🇷 Tradução | 📐 Tipo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What are the exact steps to reproduce this defect?** | *uót ar dhi egzæct stêps tchu riprôudiús dis dífêct?* | Quais são os passos exatos para reproduzir este defeito? | Wh- Question |
| **What is the expected versus actual behavior?** | *uót iz dhi ekspéctid vârsâs éktchual bi-rêiviôr?* | Qual é o comportamento esperado versus o comportamento real? | Wh- Question |
| **Does this bug reproduce in the production environment?** | *dâz dis bág riprôudiús in dhâ prôudákxân inváirônmênt?* | Esse bug se reproduz no ambiente de produção? | Yes/No (Present Simple - Does) |
| **How many users or API requests are affected?** | *ráu méni iúzerz ór êi-pi-ái rikuésts ar aféctid?* | Quantos usuários ou requisições de API são afetados? | How many (Countable) |
| **Why wasn't this caught in our automated regression suite?** | *uái uózânt dis cót in áuâr ótômêitid rigréshân suít?* | Por que isso não foi pego na nossa suíte de regressão automatizada? | Negative Wh- Question |

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Study Plan

### 📌 Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2): Build Your Verb & Grammar Base

**Goal:** Internalize the 200 most common verbs with correct pronunciation and master how to ask questions.

**Step 1 — Open [200_verbs_english.MD](200_verbs_english.MD)**

- 📖 Study **20 verbs per day** — one full section at a time
- 🗣️ Read each verb **aloud** using the phonetic guide
- 🧠 Cover the table and try to recall the **past tense from memory**
- ✍️ Write 1–2 sentences using the verb in an IT work context

**Step 2 — Study [questions.md](questions.md) for Asking Questions**

- ❓ Practice forming **Yes/No**, **Wh-**, and **Modal questions** out loud using the phonetic guide
- 🔄 Convert statements into questions (e.g., *"The pipeline failed."* ➔ *"Why did the pipeline fail?"*)

**Step 3 — Apply Spaced Repetition**

- 🔁 Review on days: **1 → 2 → 5 → 10 → 20**
- 🗂️ Create flashcards in Anki or Quizlet (front = verb/question, back = translation + phonetics)
- 🧪 Test yourself without looking at the table (active recall)

**Step 4 — Build a Daily Habit**

- ⏰ Study **20–30 minutes every morning** before work — consistency is more powerful than long sessions
- 📝 Keep a notebook or digital doc to log verbs and questions you confuse or hesitate on

---

### 📌 Phase 2 — Technical English (Weeks 3–4): Learn IT & QA Specific Vocabulary

**Goal:** Expand vocabulary to cover your real daily software testing and engineering work.

**Step 1 — Switch to [200_verbs_it_english.MD](200_verbs_it_english.MD)**

- 🎯 Prioritize sections matching your current role:
  - 🧪 **QA & SDET?** Focus on Testing & QA (41–60) and Version Control (81–100)
  - ⚙️ **DevOps & CI/CD?** Focus on DevOps/CI-CD (61–80) and Cloud (161–180)
  - 💻 **Backend / Fullstack?** Focus on Programming (21–40) and Database (101–120)
- 💡 For each verb, recall a **real situation from your last sprint** and form an English sentence

**Step 2 — Practice Career & Action Verbs**

- 🎯 Open [interview_verbs_career.MD](interview_verbs_career.MD) and study 10 verbs per day
- 🗣️ For each verb, complete this impact template:
  > *"I [verb] the [thing] by [method], which resulted in [outcome]."*  
  > *Example:* *"I **reduced** test flakiness by 60% by implementing explicit waits across the suite."*

**Step 3 — Write in English at Work**

- ⌨️ Write all **Git commit messages** in English
- 🎫 Write **Jira/YouTrack tickets**, bug reports, and acceptance criteria in English
- 💬 Write **Slack/Teams messages** to teammates in English whenever possible

---

### 📌 Phase 3 — Interview Preparation (Weeks 5–6): Simulate Real QA Interviews

**Goal:** Be able to answer any manual, automation, or process interview question fluently and confidently in English.

**Step 1 — Study Manual Testing Interview Scenarios**

- 📋 Open [interview_qa_manual_testing.MD](interview_qa_manual_testing.MD)
- 🎙️ Pick **3 questions per day**, formulate your answer in English, then say it aloud without reading
- 📱 Record yourself on your phone and listen back — identify weak vocabulary or hesitations

**Step 2 — Study Automation & DevOps Technical Interview Topics**

- ⚙️ Open [interview_qa_automation_devops.MD](interview_qa_automation_devops.MD)
- 🔍 Focus on sections matching your tech stack (Playwright, Cypress, Appium, CI/CD, Docker)
- 📊 Personalize the model answers with your **real project details and metrics**

**Step 3 — Full Dialogue Simulation**

- 🎭 Open [tips_english_interview_qa.MD](tips_english_interview_qa.MD)
- 🗣️ Read both the **interviewer and candidate roles aloud** in a single session
- 🤝 Practice mock interviews with a peer or mentor using the questions in this guide

**Goal:** Actively use English every day across multiple real channels.

**Step 1 — Write Daily Status Reports**

## 💡 Pro Tips for IT & QA Professionals

### 🔑 High-Impact Daily Habits

- ⌨️ **Write all commit messages in English** — even simple ones like `fix: handle null token in auth middleware` or `test: add e2e coverage for checkout payment flow` train your brain naturally
- 🐛 **Write Jira/YouTrack bug tickets in English** — structure with *Title*, *Environment*, *Steps to Reproduce*, *Expected Result*, and *Actual Result*
- 🤖 **Use Grammarly, DeepL Write, or LanguageTool** when writing emails or Slack messages — always review the suggestions to understand the grammar reasoning
- 📖 **Read official documentation directly in English** — Playwright, Appium, Cypress, Robot Framework, Docker, Kubernetes — eliminate automatic browser translations
- 🧠 **Think directly in English** — when forming a question or status update in a meeting, assemble the phrase in English using the structures in [questions.md](questions.md) before translating

### 🗣️ Speaking Improvement Tricks

- 🎙️ **Record your voice** answering QA interview questions from [interview_qa_manual_testing.MD](interview_qa_manual_testing.MD) and listen back — note hesitations and filler words
- 👥 **Shadow native speakers** — pause YouTube technical talks or podcasts and repeat the sentence with identical rhythm and intonation (3 repetitions)
- ⭐ **Apply the STAR method** to every behavioral answer: **S**ituation ➔ **T**ask ➔ **A**ction ➔ **R**esult
- 🚀 **Practice imperfect English daily** — consistent speaking practice beats waiting for "perfection"

### 📝 Writing & Communication Practice

- 📝 **Write a 3-sentence daily work log in English** — what you completed, what you're working on, and any blockers
- 👔 **Translate and maintain your LinkedIn profile in English** — highlight achievements with action verbs from [interview_verbs_career.MD](interview_verbs_career.MD)
- 🔀 **Write detailed Pull Request descriptions in English** — outline what was changed, why it matters, and exact test steps to verify

### 🎧 Listening & Immersion Tricks

- 🎬 **Turn on English subtitles** for movies, series, and tech videos — train both your ear and reading recognition simultaneously
- 🎧 **Listen to tech podcasts daily (15–20 min)** while commuting or exercising — e.g., *Syntax.fm*, *Software Engineering Daily*, *The Changelog*, *Lex Fridman*
- 🌐 **Follow international QA Leads and SDETs on LinkedIn/X** — read discussions, industry posts, and comment in English
